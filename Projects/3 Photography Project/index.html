About Founder

Alec soth is an American photographer, based in Minneapolis. Soth make "large-scale American projects" featuring the mid western United States. New York Times art critic Hilarie M. Sheets wrote that he has made a "photographic career out of finding chemistry with strangers" and photographs "loners and dreamers".
